From survivor to saviour: A journey of beating childhood cancer and giving back to the society

A successful story of Robert Marine, a childhood cancer survivor and founder of Champions of Hope, a Community-Based Organization.His journey is unique;from surviving cancer to helping others demonstrates his commitment to giving back to society.

Hope in the face of adversity

Speranza Maina,a breast cancer survior narrates her ordeal.Her advice to cancer patient is that having cancer is not a death sentence. ‘‘People think that if you have cancer you are going to die but through my experience you can live for so many years. Self-acceptance, listening to your doctor, following medication, attending the clinics, taking your medication, and above all trusting in God are some of the ways that I have made it this far.” She recounts. live preview
